# Calculator

## What is the "Output Spread" column?

The "Output Spread" column is the bid-ask spread of the output. It is calculated with the following formula: `Output Spread = (Output Ask - Output Bid) / Output Ask`. Possible values range from 0 to 100%. Values close to zero suggest that the output is very liquid, while values close to 100% suggest that the output is very illiquid.

# Market

## What is "3 Day p90 Prices" and "3 Day p10 Prices"?

"3 Day p90 Prices" is the 90th percentile of the prices of the market prices over the last 3 days. "3 Day p10 Prices" is the 10th percentile of the prices of the market prices over the last 3 days. These options are useful to understand the long-term market prices. When using these options, it is recommended to use less conservative values on the bid-ask sliders, as the market prices are more stable.
